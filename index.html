Why do official public lineups begin at U11/U12?

In accordance with DBU Children's Football (Børnefodbold) regulations for U5–U10, official electronic match sheets and league tables are not published publicly to prioritize child welfare, player development, and data privacy. Beginning at U11/U12 and U13 Liga Øst, complete match sheets, lineups, and final scores are officially published by DBU.
